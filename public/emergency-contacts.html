Emergency Contacts

If someone is in immediate danger, call your local emergency number first.

Important

This page is informational and may not be complete. Always follow official guidance and local authorities.

Lebanon (commonly used)

  • 112 — Police / ISF emergency
  • 140 — Lebanese Red Cross ambulance

If you know a correction to these numbers, message us on Support.

Pastoral emergencies

For urgent pastoral needs (e.g., anointing of the sick), contact the nearest parish office or bishopric. If you don’t have a number, use Support and we’ll help route you.

Safety note

If you’re traveling to a parish you don’t know, confirm the address and service time before leaving, and share your plan with a friend or family member.
